For parents and carers

We collect nothing.

499apps Games has no accounts, no ads, no analytics, no cookies and no trackers. Your child can play without anyone watching.

What the games do not do

  • They do not ask for a name, an e-mail address, a photo or any other personal information.
  • They do not show ads and do not sell anything.
  • They do not use analytics, cookies, trackers or fingerprinting.
  • They do not link to other websites.
  • They make no network calls after the page loads. The only download is the site's own files, so the games work offline.
  • They do not use chat, accounts or sharing.

What stays on this device

Some small settings and scores stay in this browser's local storage. They never leave this device. Every key starts with g499:.

KeyWhat it holds
g499:muteSound on or off, for all games
g499:snake:*Snake: best score and settings
g499:coloring:*Coloring: saved pictures and settings

Keys on this device now: none

Clear my data

Hold the button for about one second. This removes every g499: key from this browser. The games still work offline afterwards.

Works offline

After the first visit, the browser keeps a copy of the site's own files. The games then work in airplane mode. This copy holds no personal data.

Children's privacy (COPPA)

The games do not collect, use or share personal information from children or from anyone else. Because nothing is collected, there is nothing to ask consent for, to review or to delete on a server. This makes the games COPPA-friendly by design.
